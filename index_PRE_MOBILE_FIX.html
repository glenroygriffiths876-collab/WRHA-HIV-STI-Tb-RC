Programme monitoring

Priority programme targets

Quick operational reminders for Contact Investigation monitoring. Targets below are kept distinct from longer-term aspirations.

≥90%

STI / FP HIV testing · Month 6

Review eligible STI/FP attendees who did not receive HIV testing and document the reason/action.

Month 3 ≥75%; Month 6 ≥90%; thereafter progress toward 100%.
≥80%

Hospital PITC · Month 6

Use eligible admissions as the denominator and map missed opportunities by ward/service/shift.

Provide feedback to clinicians on missed opportunities.
STI

Facility + Clinic documentation

Record the actual facility and enter the clinic/service as STI where the attendance is at the STI clinic.

Parish CI/DIO oversight should check completeness and missed-test follow-up.
Guide

Syndromic management

Use the national STI manual for syndrome-based assessment, including candidiasis/yeast and bacterial vaginosis where clinically applicable.

Open the full manual for exact diagnostic and treatment guidance.
