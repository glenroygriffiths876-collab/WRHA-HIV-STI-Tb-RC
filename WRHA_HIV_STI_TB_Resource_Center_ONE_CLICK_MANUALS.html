Programme monitoring

Priority programme targets

Operational reminders for Contact Investigation monitoring. These distinguish programme trajectory from longer-term aspiration so the dashboard does not misstate targets.

≥90%

STI / FP HIV testing by Month 6

Review every eligible STI/FP attendee who did not receive HIV testing and document the reason/action.

Progress thereafter toward 100%.
≥80%

Hospital PITC by Month 6

Track eligible hospital admissions, map missed opportunities by ward/service/shift, and provide clinician feedback.

Use eligible admissions as the denominator.
100%

STI syndromic management review

Use the national STI guidance for common syndromes, including vaginal discharge causes such as candidiasis/yeast and bacterial vaginosis where clinically applicable.

Use the clinical manual for diagnosis/treatment details.
STI

Facility + Clinic documentation

When the prescribed reporting form asks for Facility and Clinic, identify the facility and record the clinic/service as STI where that is the actual service.

Parish CI/DIO oversight should check completeness and missed-test follow-up.
